FAQ

Applicant questions

Do I need consulting experience?

No. Most applicants will be new to risk consulting. We care more about curiosity and care with detail than past experience.

Which majors can apply?

All of them. Risk consulting firms hire from Computer Science, Economics, AEM, Government, ORIE, Statistics and more.

How will I hear back?

By email, after each step. Everyone who applies hears back.
